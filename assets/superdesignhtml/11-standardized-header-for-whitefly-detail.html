Whitefly

Whitefly

Bemisia tabaci

High Risk

Tiny sap-sucking insects that multiply rapidly in warm greenhouse environments. They weaken plants and transmit viral diseases while excreting sticky honeydew.

Neem oil spray
Insecticidal soaps
Yellow sticky traps
Reflective mulch
Prevention
01

Inspect new plants

Check undersides of leaves before bringing inside to avoid contamination.

02

Encourage predators

Ladybugs and lacewings are natural enemies that keep whitefly populations down.

03

Use reflective mulch

Deters flying insects from landing on crops by confusing their orientation.

04

Maintain airflow

Prevents stagnant humid air where pests thrive. Open greenhouse vents daily.
