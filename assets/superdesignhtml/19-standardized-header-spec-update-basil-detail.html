Growing Tips

Provide at least 6-8 hours of direct sun daily. Rotate pot weekly for even growth.

Keep soil consistently moist but not soggy. Basil wilts quickly if it dries out.

Pinch off flower spikes as they appear to preserve leaf flavor and plant energy.

Mulch with organic matter to retain soil temperature and humidity in Papamoa summers.

Harvesting Guide
1

Start harvesting when plant has at least 6 leaves. Always pick from the top down.

2

Cut the stem just above the point where two large leaves meet. This encourages branching.

3

Harvest regularly to keep the plant productive and prevent it from going to seed.
