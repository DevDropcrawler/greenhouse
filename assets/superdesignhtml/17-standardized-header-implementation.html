Botrytis

Botrytis cinerea

High Impact

Known as 'Gray Mold', this fungal disease thrives in humid conditions. It spreads via spores and can quickly devastate fruit, flowers, and foliage in poorly ventilated greenhouses.

Symptoms
  • Wet brown spots on petals and leaves
  • Fuzzy gray mold layer (sporulation)
  • Soft rot of fruit and stems
  • Rapid defoliation and decay
Damage looks like
Improve air circulation
Remove and burn infected parts
Avoid overhead watering
Prevention
01

Maximize Airflow

Use greenhouse fans and vent daily to keep humidity below 60%.

02

Clean Pruning

Sanitize secateurs between every cut to prevent cross-contamination.

03

Morning Watering

Water only in the morning so foliage dries before evening humidity rises.

Affected Crops
Strawberry 'Camarosa' Tomato 'Money Maker' Meyer Lemon Capsicum
